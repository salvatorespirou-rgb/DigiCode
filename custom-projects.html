Custom Projects

Sites we're building ourselves.

Original websites built in-house, on our own time — ready to hand over to the right buyer instead of starting from scratch.

Unlike a custom build made for one client, these are projects we design and develop ourselves first — a finished (or near-finished) site you can acquire outright, often faster and cheaper than commissioning something new.

We're currently building our first projects for this page — check back soon to see what's available.

How Buying A Finished Site Works

Most web work starts with an empty folder and a three-to-six week wait. These don't. They're complete sites we've already designed and built — you look at the finished thing, decide if it fits, and take it over. Nobody is describing a website to you and asking you to imagine it.

What you're actually buying

The whole site and everything under it. Not a licence, not a subscription — the code becomes yours the day it changes hands.

  • Every page, hand-coded and already working
  • The design files and image assets
  • Your business name, wording and photos swapped in
  • Domain and hosting set up, or moved to accounts you own
  • Full ownership — no monthly fee to keep it running

Why it costs less

The expensive part of a website isn't the code, it's the back-and-forth. Discovery calls, design rounds, revisions, sign-offs. On these, that work is already done.

  • No discovery phase — the site already exists
  • No design rounds — you can see exactly what you're getting
  • Live in days rather than weeks
  • A fixed price with nothing added later

Who it suits

It's the right call when you need to be online properly and soon, and the site type is a well-understood shape.

  • A new business that needs to look established from day one
  • Replacing a slow, dated or half-finished site
  • Trades, salons, cafés, gyms — anyone whose customers check online first
  • Creators wanting a home base off the big platforms

What happens after

It's a real site, not a template you're stuck with. Anything on it can be changed, because it's actual code.

  • Add pages, features or a shop whenever you want
  • Hosting, updates and SEO management if you want them handled
  • A portal and a direct line to your developer
  • Take it to another developer any time — nothing is locked

Ready-Made Or Built For You?

Buy one of these if the shape fits and you want to move quickly — you save weeks and a good chunk of the cost. Commission a custom build if you need something the shelf doesn't hold: a booking system tied to your existing software, a members area, a shop with unusual rules, a streaming or gifting platform. Both end up as hand-written code you own outright. The only real difference is how much of it existed before you arrived.

Want something built from the ground up instead? Start a custom request.
