What We Do

Technical SEO Audits

The 110+ point check that everything else is built on. We find what's actually holding the site back before anyone writes a word of content.

  • Crawlability and indexation
  • Page speed and Core Web Vitals
  • Mobile usability and layout stability
  • Broken links, redirect chains and error pages
  • Duplicate content and canonical tags
  • Schema markup and structured data
  • Sitemaps, robots and crawl directives
  • HTTPS, security headers and site health

Keyword Tracking & Coverage

Up to 20,000 keywords a day, checked daily rather than monthly, so we see a drop the week it happens instead of the quarter after.

  • Daily rank tracking across your whole keyword set
  • Local, national and map-pack positions
  • Competitor position monitoring
  • Gap analysis — what they rank for and you don't
  • Search intent mapping across the buying journey
  • Movement alerts when something shifts

Content Optimisation

Making the pages you already have work harder, and filling the gaps where your market is searching and you're not showing up.

  • On-page optimisation across every key page
  • Titles, meta descriptions and heading structure
  • Internal linking so your best pages get the weight
  • Content gap analysis and refresh planning
  • Image compression, naming and alt text
  • Readability, structure and conversion copy

Influencer & Creator Management

For creators, your website is the one platform that can't be taken off you. We make it the hub everything else points at.

  • Profile, bio and cross-platform link strategy
  • Landing pages built to convert followers
  • Sponsorship and media-kit pages for brand deals
  • Content calendar and posting support
  • Analytics pulled together across every platform
  • Streaming, memberships and tipping built in
