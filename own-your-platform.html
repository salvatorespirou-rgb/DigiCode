Own Your Platform

Stop renting your audience.

Right now your channel lives on someone else's land. They set the rules, they own the relationship with your viewers, and they take a slice of everything you make. One website changes all three.

Start My Platform See What's Possible
On a big platform
  • They keep a cut of subs, tips and ad money — often 30–50% on the major ones.
  • The algorithm decides who sees you. Post at the wrong hour and it's gone.
  • Your followers are their users. You can't email them.
  • A rule changes, a strike lands, and years of work can vanish overnight.
  • You look like every other channel on the site.
  • Want a new feature? Wait and hope they build it.
On your own site
  • Tips and memberships come straight to you — you keep what you earn, minus card fees.
  • No algorithm. Your people land on your page, every time.
  • Your audience list is yours. Email them whenever you like.
  • Nobody can switch it off. It's your domain and your data.
  • It looks like you — not a template with your logo dropped in.
  • Want a new feature? Message us. It gets built.

Card processing always costs something — that's the payment provider, not us, and it's a small fraction of a platform's cut. The point is simple: the middle layer disappears.

What your site could actually do

None of this is a "premium tier" you unlock later. It's just what we build. Pick the pieces you want now and add the rest whenever you're ready.

📡

Stream from your own page

Go live on your site instead of only on someone else's. Embed the stream you already run, or host it yourself — either way your viewers arrive at your address, not a channel buried in a feed.

🎁

Gifting & tips that reach you

Let people send a tip, buy you a coffee, or gift a membership to someone else — with the money landing in your account rather than being held, split and paid out on someone else's schedule.

⭐

Memberships & subscriber perks

Your own tiers, your own prices, your own perks. Members-only posts, early access, private chat, subscriber-only streams — and you decide what each level gets, not a platform's rulebook.

📱

Your own app

The same site, installable on a phone with your icon on the home screen — and a real iOS/Android build when you're ready for it. Push a notification when you go live instead of hoping the algorithm tells people.

💬

A community that's yours

Live chat during streams, a forum, comments, a members' wall. Your community stops being an account you rent and becomes something you actually own and can move.

🛍️

Merch & digital sales

Sell shirts, presets, packs, guides, tickets — whatever your thing is. No marketplace fee on top, no waiting for approval, and the checkout matches the rest of your site.

🎬

Your back catalogue, organised

Every VOD, clip and highlight in one place, sorted how you want — not dumped in a reverse-chronological list. Old work keeps earning instead of disappearing down a feed.

🔗

One home for every platform

Keep posting wherever you like — this becomes the hub they all point at. One link in every bio, and it's a real site with your name on it rather than a list of buttons on a service you don't control.

📊

Numbers you can actually see

Who's visiting, what they read, what converts — the real picture, not a dashboard built to keep you posting. It's your data, and we'll walk you through what it's telling you.

✉️

A mailing list nobody can take

The single most valuable thing on this page. Followers live on a platform; an email list lives with you. If every platform vanished tomorrow, you'd still be able to reach your people.

🗓️

Bookings, events & tickets

Coaching slots, meet-ups, watch parties, paid one-to-ones. Take the booking and the payment on your own site instead of juggling three separate tools.

♾️

Change anything, anytime

New colour, new section, whole new page, a feature you thought of in the shower — message us and it happens. No plan upgrade, no ticket queue, no "that's not supported."
