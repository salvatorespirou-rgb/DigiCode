About DigiCode

DigiCode is a web studio built on one idea: what you pay for should actually be yours. Every site is written by hand, line by line — no page builders, no bloated templates, and no platform holding the keys to your own front door.

We don't stop at brochure sites. We build business sites and online stores, creator platforms with streaming, memberships and tipping built in, SEO and hosting, and full games we can ship as real apps on iOS and Android. If it runs in a browser or on a phone, it's on the table — and it costs a fraction of what an agency would quote you for far less.

You deal directly with the person building your site. Your portal shows exactly where the project's up to, how your site is actually performing, and a message thread straight to your developer. Think of something you want changed a year from now? Message us. It gets built — no upgrade, no ticket queue, no "that's not supported."

Too many good businesses and creators are renting space on someone else's platform, paying a cut of everything they earn and living at the mercy of an algorithm. We'd rather hand you the keys.

The Team

DigiCode is a small, hands-on team — which is why you deal directly with the person doing the work rather than an account handler. Between us, this is what we bring to a project.

  • Front-end & back-end development — every site hand-coded from scratch, no page builders or bought templates
  • Technical SEO — crawl, index, speed and structure work on live production sites
  • Content strategy & copywriting — writing that ranks and still sounds like you
  • Analytics & reporting — tracking set up properly, reported in plain English
  • E-commerce & payments — online stores, checkouts and secure payment handling
  • App development — real apps shipped to iOS and Android, not web pages in a wrapper
  • Game development — full browser games built in-house and playable on our own site
  • Creator & streaming platforms — streaming, gifting and membership systems you own outright
  • Hosting, domains & servers — the whole stack managed so you're not chasing three providers
  • Brand & graphic design — logos, identity and the look that carries across everything
  • Direct client communication — a message thread straight to your developer, no ticket queue
