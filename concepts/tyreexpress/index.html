Tyre Size

Reading the numbers on your tyre.

It is printed on the sidewall of every tyre on the car. Take a photo of it and we can have the right one ready before you arrive.

195 Section width
The width of the tyre in millimetres, measured sidewall to sidewall at its widest point.
65 Aspect ratio
The sidewall height as a percentage of the width. Here the sidewall is 65% of 195 mm, so about 127 mm tall.
R Construction
Radial — the way the tyre is built. Almost every car tyre on the road today is radial.
15 Rim diameter
The wheel size in inches that the tyre is made to fit.
91V Load & speed
The weight each tyre can carry and the speed rating it is certified to. Match or better what the car came with.
