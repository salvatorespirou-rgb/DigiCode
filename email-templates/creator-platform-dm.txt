CREATOR / STREAMER OUTREACH — direct message
============================================

For approaching someone who already has an audience on TikTok, Twitch, Kick,
Instagram or similar. Swap {{Name}} and send as a DM, not an email — these
land better in the place they already talk to people.

The argument is not "your site could look nicer". It is: you built the
audience, someone else is charging you to reach it.

Do not oversell "100%". Card processing takes roughly 1.75% + 30c in
Australia, and a creator who has been paid out before will know that. Saying
"everything except the card fee" is both true and more convincing than a round
number.

IF THEY ASK THE OBVIOUS QUESTIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

"Who handles the payments?"
  Stripe, in your name, paying into your account. Money never touches me.

"What about the app stores / moderation?"
  It's a website, so no app store cut and no approval process. Moderation
  tools are part of the build - you decide the rules, not a platform.

"Won't people just stay on TikTok?"
  Most will. The point isn't replacing it - it's having somewhere to send the
  ones who'd pay you properly, and owning that relationship. Keep posting where
  the audience is, monetise where you keep it.

"What if I want to leave?"
  You own the code outright. Take it to any developer, any time.
