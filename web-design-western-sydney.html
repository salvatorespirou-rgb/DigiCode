Out here almost nobody finds you from a desk. Why Speed Matters More Out Here Than Anywhere

A typical WordPress trade site runs fifteen to twenty plugins and takes five to eight seconds to load on 4G. Google's own research puts the bounce rate at over 50% once you pass three seconds — so before your site has even finished drawing, half the people who searched for you have gone back and tapped the next result. Our builds carry no plugins because they're written by hand, and they load in about a second on the same connection. For a trade business that's not a technical detail, it's the phone ringing or not ringing.
