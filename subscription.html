Website Management

Keep your site running itself.

Ongoing care for a site that's already live — updates, design, and support on a rhythm that suits you. Pick Monthly or Weekly billing below.

Every plan includes ongoing management from a real developer, not an automated system. The difference between tiers comes down to how often we touch your site, and how much extra support comes with it.

Silver

Steady, reliable upkeep for a site that's already live. The right starting point once your build is finished and you just want it looked after — content kept current, domain handled, nothing falling through the cracks.

$80/mo
  • Monthly management check-up — we review your site every month
  • Up to 5 content updates per page, every month
  • Domain managed and renewed by the DigiCode domain team
  • Direct email support for update requests
  • Basic uptime monitoring
Gold

More updates, real design support, and a photographer in your corner. Built for businesses whose site needs to keep evolving, not just stay online — new content, fresh visuals, and faster turnaround on requests.

$125/mo
  • Monthly management check-up, with the option to upgrade to weekly
  • Up to 10 content updates per page, every month
  • Domain managed and renewed by the DigiCode domain team
  • Graphic design included — no need to source your own images
  • 1 hour of professional photography included
  • Priority email support
  • Uptime monitoring with alerts
Platinum

Everything, on a weekly rhythm — the full DigiCode team behind your site. For businesses that treat their website as core infrastructure, not an afterthought.

$500/mo
  • Weekly management check-ups as standard
  • Unlimited content updates
  • Domain managed and renewed by the DigiCode domain team
  • Graphic design included — no need to source your own images
  • 1 hour of professional photography included
  • SEO monitoring included
  • Website hosting included
  • Priority support and faster turnaround on every request
  • Direct line to your assigned developer
