Blue Mountains

Web design in the Blue Mountains.

Websites for accommodation, hospitality and makers across Katoomba, Leura, Blackheath, Springwood and the villages — built light, because the reception up here isn't.

Mountains businesses have a problem the rest of Sydney doesn't: a good share of your visitors are already up here, on one or two bars, trying to find somewhere to eat or sleep tonight. A heavy site simply doesn't load for them. Everything we build is hand-coded and light enough to open on a bad connection — which up here is worth more than any design flourish.

Who We Build For In The Mountains

Accommodation & stays

Cabins, cottages, BnBs and guesthouses. Every booking taken on your own site is a booking you keep all of.

  • Direct booking, so no 15–18% platform commission
  • Availability calendars and seasonal pricing
  • Photo galleries that stay fast on a weak signal
  • Deposits and payments taken securely
  • Guest information, check-in details and local guides

Cafés, restaurants & venues

Weekend trade swings hard on weather and school holidays. The site needs to answer "are you open and can I get a table" instantly.

  • Table bookings without a per-cover fee
  • Menus you can change yourself, not a PDF from 2023
  • Seasonal hours that are actually current
  • Function and wedding enquiry pages

Wedding & event venues

A high-value, heavily researched booking. Couples will compare you against a dozen venues, mostly at night, mostly on a phone.

  • Galleries that load properly on mobile data
  • Packages, capacities and pricing laid out clearly
  • Enquiry forms that capture the date and guest count
  • Supplier lists and downloadable information packs

Artists, makers & galleries

The mountains are full of people making genuinely good work and selling it through someone else's marketplace.

  • An online shop that takes no cut of your sales
  • Portfolio and exhibition pages
  • Commission and enquiry flows
  • Workshop and class bookings

Tourists Search Differently

Someone in Katoomba for the weekend isn't searching the way a local does. They search "things to do Blue Mountains", "dog friendly cabin Blackheath", "breakfast Leura" — long, specific, and heavily weighted to whatever's nearby right now. That means proper local search setup, pages that match how visitors actually phrase things, and a Google Business Profile kept current with seasonal hours. It also means the site has to open on a phone halfway down a walking track. We build for that first and make it look good second — though it does both.

Running something in the mountains? We come up, and the price is fixed before we start.
